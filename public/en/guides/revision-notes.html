How to make effective revision notes

By Kelvyn Laine, creator of Abyssia · updated October 30, 2026

In short: Good revision notes fit on one page, keep only the essentials (definitions, key concepts, formulas, examples) and are written in your own words, from memory as much as possible. You then use them to quiz yourself: cover the content and try to recall it, instead of rereading it.

Why make revision notes?

Making a revision sheet forces you to sort: what you absolutely must know, and what is a detail. That work of selecting and rephrasing is already part of learning. Notes copied word for word from the lesson are worth much less: they become a second copy of the lesson, not a revision tool.

What to put on a revision sheet

  • The chapter title and its central question: "Linear functions: how do you recognise and draw a straight line?".
  • Definitions, written simply.
  • Concepts, dates or formulas to know by heart, made to stand out.
  • A short example for each abstract idea: a calculation, a quotation, a concrete case.
  • Traps you have already fallen into in exercises or that your teacher pointed out.

The method in 4 steps

  1. Read the chapter once from start to finish, without writing anything.
  2. Close the book and write down from memory what you remember: that's the draft of your notes.
  3. Open the book again to fill the gaps, in a different colour: those are your weak spots.
  4. Write a clean version on a single page, with short headings, lists and arrows rather than paragraphs.

The Cornell template

The Cornell method splits the page into three areas: a narrow column on the left for questions or keywords, a large area on the right for the notes, and a two- or three-sentence summary at the bottom. To revise, you cover the right-hand side and answer the questions in the left-hand column.

Using your notes

A revision sheet is useful when it makes you think. Read the question or heading, try to say the content out loud, then check. Anything you forgot can become flashcards, and the reviews of each sheet should be spread over several days (see a four-week revision plan).
