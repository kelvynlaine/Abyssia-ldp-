Guides to studying effectively

Simple, proven study methods — testing yourself, spacing out your reviews, working in short sessions — and how to apply them with AI.

  • How to study with AI: a 5-step method
    How to revise effectively with artificial intelligence: have it write a lesson, test yourself with quizzes and flashcards, and plan short study sessions.
  • The Pomodoro technique for studying: a practical guide
    The Pomodoro technique applied to revision: 25 minutes of work, a 5-minute break, a longer break after four rounds. How to use it and the mistakes to avoid.
  • Flashcards and spaced repetition: remember for the long term
    Why flashcards and spaced repetition work for memorising, how to write good cards and how often to review them before an exam.
  • How to make effective revision notes
    How to make good revision notes: what to put on them, the structure to follow, the Cornell method and how to use them to test yourself.
  • Revising with a mind map: how to do it
    How to make a mind map to revise a chapter: central idea, branches, keywords, colours. Which subjects it works best for.
  • Exam revision plan: a 4-week schedule
    How to organise your revision for final exams (A-levels, the French baccalauréat, university finals) over four weeks: take stock, spread the chapters, space out reviews and practise past papers.
  • Learning foreign language vocabulary: the method that works
    How to learn and remember vocabulary in French, Spanish, Italian or any other language: words in context, two-way flashcards, spaced repetition, using words in speaking and writing.
  • Revising the night before an exam: what to do (and avoid)
    How to use the night before an exam: go over your notes and past mistakes, do a quick self-test, get your things ready and sleep well rather than revising all night.
